# Country Smart AI — Website Revision Request Form

The second Google Form. Its whole job is to stop revisions arriving as a text on Tuesday, an email on Wednesday and their sister's opinion on Sunday night.

Send this link at the same time as the preview link, not after the changes start coming in.

**Form title:** Country Smart AI Website Revision Request

**Form description:**

> Your package includes one revision round. Please review the entire website — on your phone and on a computer — and send all your requested changes together through this form.
>
> Submit one entry per change. There's no limit on how many entries you send, as long as they all come through in this round.
>
> Have a look with anyone whose opinion matters to you *before* you submit, so we can get it all done in one go.

---

## Questions

| # | Question | Type | Required |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Your name | Short answer | Yes |
| 2 | Business name | Short answer | Yes |
| 3 | Which page? | Multiple choice + Other | Yes |
| 4 | Which section of the page? | Short answer | Yes |
| 5 | Current text or content | Paragraph | No |
| 6 | What would you like it changed to? | Paragraph | Yes |
| 7 | Screenshot | **File upload** | No |
| 8 | Other notes | Paragraph | No |

**Q3 options:** Home / About / Services / Menu / Contact / Gallery / Testimonials / FAQs / Applies to the whole site / Other

**Q4 help text:** For example: the top banner, the third box down, the footer, the contact form.

**Q5 help text:** Copy and paste what's on the site now, so I know exactly which bit you mean.

**Q6 help text:** Be as specific as you can. "Change the phone number to 0400 000 000" is easier to action than "the contact bit is wrong".

**Q7 help text:** A screenshot with a circle or arrow on it is the fastest way to show me something. Optional, but it helps.

---

## Form settings

- **Allow response editing:** off
- **Show link to submit another response:** **on** — this is the key setting. It lets them log change after change without reopening the link.
- **Confirmation message:** *"Got it. Add another change using the link below, or if that's everything, sit tight — I'll work through the list and send you the updated site."*
- **Link responses to a spreadsheet:** yes. Sort by page, work down the list, tick them off.

---

Paste this into the email or message with the preview link:

> Here's your website preview: [link]
>
> Have a proper look through on your phone and on a computer. Click every button, read every page, check your contact details and pricing carefully.
>
> Your package includes one revision round, so please send **all** your changes together using this form: [revision form link]
>
> One change per entry, as many entries as you need. Take your time and show anyone whose opinion matters to you before you submit — once we've worked through the list, any further changes are quoted separately.
>
> Anything you're unsure about, just ask.
