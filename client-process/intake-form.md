# Country Smart AI — Website Client Intake Form

The full question list for the Google Form, section by section. Two ways to build it:

- **Fast:** run `create-forms.gs` (see [README](README.md)) and it builds the whole thing for you.
- **By hand:** copy each question below into Google Forms. Use **Add section** at every "Section N" heading so it arrives one page at a time instead of one giant scroll.

**Form title:** Country Smart AI Website Client Intake Form

**Form description:**

> Thanks for choosing Country Smart AI. This form gathers everything I need to build your website. It takes about 15–20 minutes. Rough notes are completely fine — you don't need polished wording. You can come back to it later if you need to gather photos or details.

## Form settings

- **Progress bar:** on
- **Confirmation message:** *"Thanks — got it. I'll review everything and get back to you within 2 business days if I need anything else. If you've got photos or files still to send, email them through whenever you're ready."*
- **Collect email addresses:** optional (you're already asking in Q3)
- **Response receipts:** on, so they have a copy of what they sent
- **Link responses to a spreadsheet:** yes — makes the build checklist easier to fill from

## A note on file uploads

File upload questions require the person filling in the form to be signed in to a Google account, and the files land in your Google Drive. Two things to watch:

1. Keep an eye on your Drive storage if you're taking a lot of photo uploads.
2. If a client can't sign in to Google, give them the fallback: email the files, or share a Drive/Dropbox link in the notes field.
