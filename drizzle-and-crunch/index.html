Our story

Family made. Community loved.

We're Amy and Chris. We both grew up in Mudgee, and there wasn't a dessert truck in town — so we started one. That's where Drizzle & Crunch came from.

It's a family business, run out of one van. Our two kids are in it with us most weekends, taking orders and working the register. We want them to be proud of it.

Stick waffles, strawberry cups, soft serve and açaí bowls, all made to order at the window.

Being from here is most of it. Most weekends we're serving people we know.

Always with a little extra drizzle and crunch.

Weddings & private events

Park the dessert bar at your thing.

The van turns up fully branded, fully self-powered, and serves your guests all night. Weddings, birthdays, school fêtes, sports days, work Christmas parties — if there's somewhere to park it, we can bring it.

  • Van setup and pack-down
  • Generator power — no need for a plug
  • Staff for the service window
  • Public liability insurance
  • Cups, napkins, cutlery and sticks
  • Travel included within 30km of Mudgee

Dessert bar packages are priced per head, built off the shop menu — cones, sundaes, waffles, açaí or strawberry cups. Tell us the date and rough guest numbers and we'll send the full pricing guide back.
