# Restore Massage and Beauty — Wix build guide (for Lauren)

Everything below is copy-and-paste ready. Build it in the **Wix Editor** on the
**Restore Massage And** site (restorebeautyandmassage.com.au). Bookings are already
set up, so the "Book" buttons just need to link to your booking page.

Work top to bottom. Each section = one Wix "Section" (Add → Section → blank).

Link → Page → your "Book Online" / booking page).

## 6. The Menu  ← use the Wix Bookings element (important + easy)
Instead of typing the price list by hand, add the **Wix Bookings "Service List"** element
(Add → Bookings → Service List). It automatically shows every service, duration, price and a
**Book** button, pulling live from the bookings you already set up. Put a heading above it:
- Label: `THE MENU` · Heading: **Massage & Beauty**
- Intro: *All treatments are individually tailored to you. Choose a time that suits and book online.*

*(If you'd rather show a printed-style menu instead, the full list with prices is in
`BOOKING_SETUP.md`.)*

## 10. FAQ (Wix "FAQ" element or accordions)
Heading: **Frequently asked.**
- **Where are you located?** — Restore is at 85 Market Street, Mudgee, inside and upstairs of Mixi Blu. Head in and up the stairs, and you'll find us. Street parking is available nearby along Market Street.
- **How do I book?** — The quickest way is online through the Book Now button. You'll see live availability and can secure your spot in a couple of taps. Payment is taken at the time of booking.
- **What's your cancellation policy?** — Life happens, so we just ask for at least 24 hours' notice if you need to change or cancel, so the time can be offered to someone else.
- **What should I expect at my first visit?** — Arrive a few minutes early to settle in. Lauren will chat with you about what you're after and any areas of focus, then it's over to you to relax and enjoy.
- **Do you sell gift vouchers?** — Yes! Restore vouchers can be used on any treatment and make a lovely gift. Just ask, or send an enquiry.
