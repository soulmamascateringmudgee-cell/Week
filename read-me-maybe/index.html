Questions before the first date

What if I've already read it?

Tell us at checkout — there's a box for anything you've already read or would rather avoid. If we get it wrong anyway, message us and we'll sort out the next one.

Are the books new?

Yes. Every book is new and chosen by hand.

When will it arrive?

One-off parcels are posted within 3–5 business days. Subscriptions go out in the first week of each month.

Can I change my vibe on a subscription?

Absolutely. Message us any time before the next parcel goes out and we'll switch it over.

How do I cancel a subscription?

There's a link in your Stripe receipt email to manage or cancel your plan, or just message us and we'll take care of it. No lock-in.

Do you post outside Australia?

Not yet — Australia-wide for now. Drop us a message if you'd like that to change.
