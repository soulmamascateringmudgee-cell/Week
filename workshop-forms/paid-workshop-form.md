## What to do with the answers

**A week out.** Read Q10 for everyone and group the answers. If six of eight people want help writing social posts, that's your longest module, whatever you had planned. If one person wants something nobody else does, cover it one-on-one during the working time rather than making the room sit through it.

Q14 tells you which software to have open on the screen. Demonstrating in the tool they already use lands about ten times harder than a generic example.

Q15 tells you how fast to go and who to sit near. Anyone at 1 or 2 wants you within arm's reach when it's time to actually do something.

**The day before.** Q17 goes to whoever's catering. Q6 tells you who still owes you money — chase it before the day, not after.

**Afterwards.** Ask everyone for a testimonial while they're still warm. The people who answered Q10 with something specific are the ones whose testimonials will be worth having, because they can point at the thing they can now do.
