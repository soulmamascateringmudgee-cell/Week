# Country Smart AI Free Workshop Registration

Short on purpose. A free workshop lives or dies on how easy it is to sign up, so this is three minutes and three sections. Everything that isn't needed to get someone in the room has been left for the paid form.

**Form title:** Country Smart AI Free Workshop Registration

**Description:**

> Come and find out what AI can actually do for a small country business — in plain English, no jargon, no assumed knowledge.
>
> [Date, time and venue]
>
> This takes about 3 minutes. The questions about your experience are so I can pitch the session at the right level for the room. There is no wrong answer, and "never touched it" is a very common one.

## What to do with the answers

**Before the workshop.** Sort the responses by Q7. The shape of that column decides your first twenty minutes — if half the room has never used it, you open with what AI actually is, not with prompting technique.

Read every answer to Q10 and pick three or four that come up repeatedly. Those become your live demos. Someone watching their own problem get solved in front of them is the whole ballgame.

Q11 is the one people skip. Don't — address the two most common worries out loud, early. It buys you the room.

**After the workshop.** Everyone who ticked "Yes please" on Q16 is your list. Everyone who answered "Excited and want to get started" or "Fairly confident, want to go deeper" on Q9 is who you tell about the paid workshop first.
