# Countdown, weekly rhythm and the kit

## EVENT countdown

### T-14 — Lock the job
- Menu signed off in writing
- Deposit received / invoice raised
- Guest number estimate recorded
- Dietaries in writing — allergies vs preferences
- Venue confirmed: address, kitchen access, power, water, fridge space, gas, parking, bump-in time
- Service times locked
- Pre-orders placed: whole lamb, seafood, specialty cheese, custom cake, hire gear

### T-7 — ORDER DAY (anchor)
- Confirm working headcount — this is what you order to
- Apply buffer + 2 crew meals
- Build quantities
- Shop the pantry first — record what's already in stock before ordering
- Split the list by supplier
- One email per supplier
- Delivery date and window on every order — aim for T-2, not day-of
- Confirm hire: crockery, chafers, urns, tables, linen
- Confirm crew: who, what time, what they're wearing, who leads
- Ask suppliers to flag short supply before dispatch

### T-3 — Final numbers
- Final headcount in writing
- If the number moved more than 10%, adjust orders now — most suppliers accept changes at 48–72 hrs
- Dietary count locked and matched to specific dishes
- All supplier orders confirmed back in writing
- Run sheet drafted
- Fuel, gas bottles, ice ordered or booked

### T-2 to T-1 — Receive and prep
- Check every delivery against the order sheet before signing — weights, counts, quality, temps
- Short or wrong: call the supplier same day, adjust the menu if needed
- Marinades, sauces, dressings, slow-cook items started
- Pack the packaging/equipment kit the day before, never the morning of
- Label everything: dish, date, allergen flags
- Print menu cards, allergen cards, run sheet
- Fuel the vehicle, charge phone, test EFTPOS

### Event day — Load out
- Cold chain: eskies + ice bricks packed last, loaded last, temps logged
- Knife kit, thermometer, gloves, sanitiser, first aid on board
- Serving gear counted out loud against the list
- Client contact and venue access details in the phone
- Business cards and final invoice ready

### T+1 — Close
- Final invoice sent
- Actual quantities used vs ordered recorded
- Leftovers and waste noted
- Thank you + photo request to the client

## SERVICE weekly rhythm

Anchored on delivery days rather than a countdown.

### Order day (per delivery cycle, morning)
- Count on hand — every par item, written down
- Confirm the cover forecast for the window this delivery carries
- Par minus on hand = the order
- Check the order against fridge, freezer and dry storage capacity
- Split by supplier, send with a delivery date and window
- Flag anything on short supply so the menu can move early

### Delivery day
- Check every line against the order before signing — weights, counts, temps
- Date-label everything as it lands
- Rotate: new stock behind old, no exceptions
- Short deliveries: call same day, adjust the prep list, tell the pass

### Every trading morning
- Count prepped components
- Prep the gap to par, longest shelf life first
- Total prep hours against rostered hours before starting, not at 11am

### End of week
- Record actual covers against forecast covers
- Record waste by item
- Adjust next week's sales mix from what actually sold

Two weeks of that and the par table stops being an estimate.

## Food and dry goods

**Fresh:** proteins · produce (salad, veg, potatoes, onions, garlic, lemons, limes, herbs) · fruit · dairy (butter, milk, cream, cheeses, yoghurt, sour cream, feta, parmesan) · eggs · bread (rolls, flatbread, sourdough, buns, tortillas) · deli (cured meats, olives, marinated veg, dips)

**Pantry:** oils · vinegars, mustard · salt, pepper, spice mixes, dried herbs · rice, cous cous, quinoa, pasta · flour, sugar, cornflour · stock, tinned tomato, coconut milk, legumes · sauces (mayo, aioli base, BBQ, hot sauce, soy, tahini) · nuts, seeds, dried fruit · crackers · tea, coffee, sugar

**Dietary-specific — order separately so it never gets used up in the main cook:** GF bread, crackers, flour · dairy-free milk, vegan butter, vegan cheese · vegan protein (legumes, tofu, mushroom) · separate labelled containers

**Drinks:** water, juice, soft drink, mixers · milk for coffee · ice

## Packaging and disposables kit

**Transport and storage:** gastronorm trays + lids (count against number of dishes) · cambros / insulated boxes · eskies + ice bricks · foil, cling film, baking paper, foil trays · zip bags, vac bags, takeaway containers · labels + waterproof marker

**Service:** serving tongs, spoons, ladles (2 per dish + 4 spare) · platters, boards, bowls · chafing dishes + **chafing fuel** (2 hrs each — count it, this is the classic forgotten item) · urn / hot water · napkins · disposable plates, cutlery, cups, food boats · picks, skewers, cheese knives · menu cards + allergen cards

**Hygiene and safety:** nitrile gloves (2 boxes min) · sanitiser spray + food-safe sanitiser · chux, tea towels, paper towel · hand wash + hand sanitiser · degreaser, dish liquid, scourers · heavy-duty garbage bags + recycling · probe thermometer + temp log · first aid kit, blue bandaids, burn gel · fire blanket / extinguisher (van + gas cooking)

**Van / kiosk-specific:** gas bottles (2, one spare) · oil + oil disposal container · wraps, boats, serviettes · order pad or POS · float · EFTPOS · bin bags · hand-wash station · spare tongs and scrapers

**Crew:** aprons, cloths, hats/hairnets · crew water and crew meals · run sheet printed, one per person

## The three things that go wrong most — events

1. **Headcount moves after the order goes in.** Fix: written headcount at T-3 and a stated change cut-off in the quote. Anything after T-3 is charged.
2. **Chafing fuel, ice and gas.** Not food, so they don't make the food order, then they're missing. Fix: ticked separately from the packaging list.
3. **Dietary items get eaten by the main service.** Fix: order and pack them separately, labelled, and brief the crew that they are not general stock.
