# Event Quantities — the scaling engine

All figures are **served weight per person** unless marked raw. Convert to raw
using the yield factors.

## Buffer

- Under 40 guests: +10%
- 40–80 guests: +7%
- 80+ guests: +5%
- Always: +2 crew meals

Smaller jobs carry a bigger percentage buffer because a single big eater moves
the average much further at 20 guests than at 200.

## Protein — total served per person

| Menu weight | Total served protein per person |
|---|---|
| Light lunch / daytime | 180–200 g |
| Standard dinner | 220–250 g |
| Big feasting / long table | 280–300 g |

Divide the total by the number of proteins on the menu.
100 guests, standard dinner, 3 proteins → 240 g ÷ 3 = 80 g served each.

## Yield factors — raw weight to order

| Item | Yield | Raw per 100 g served | Multiplier |
|---|---|---|---|
| Beef brisket, slow cooked | 50% | 200 g | ×2.00 |
| Pork shoulder, pulled | 50% | 200 g | ×2.00 |
| Lamb shoulder, bone in | 45% | 220 g | ×2.20 |
| Beef roast / rump | 65% | 155 g | ×1.55 |
| Chicken thigh, boneless | 70% | 145 g | ×1.45 |
| Chicken breast, boneless | 75% | 135 g | ×1.35 |
| Whole chicken | 45% | 220 g | ×2.20 |
| Fish fillet (barramundi, salmon) | 75% | 135 g | ×1.35 |
| Whole fish | 45% | 220 g | ×2.20 |
| Prawns, shell on | 50% | 200 g | ×2.00 |
| Mince (burger, tacos, koftas) | 75% | 135 g | ×1.35 |
| Tofu / firm plant protein | 90% | 110 g | ×1.10 |
| Legumes, dried | 240% | 42 g | ×0.42 |

**Worked example — 100 guests, standard dinner, brisket + chicken thigh + barramundi:**
- 80 g served each
- Brisket: 80 × 2.00 = 160 g raw → 16 kg → **17 kg with buffer**
- Chicken thigh: 80 × 1.45 = 116 g raw → 11.6 kg → **12.5 kg with buffer**
- Barramundi fillet: 80 × 1.35 = 108 g raw → 10.8 kg → **11.5 kg with buffer**

| Guests | Brisket | Chicken thigh | Fish fillet |

| 10 | 2.0 kg | 1.5 kg | 1.4 kg |

| 50 | 9.0 kg | 6.5 kg | 6.0 kg |

Every figure here comes straight out of the formula above, so the table and the
method agree. The two crew meals matter more the smaller the job gets — at 25
guests they're 8% on top, at 100 they're 2%.

## Service-style notes

**Shared table** — platters/boards 1 per 8–10 guests; serving tongs and spoons
2 per dish; allow 15 min plating per 25 guests for a two-person team. If a
shared main covers a dietary (fish covering pescatarian + GF), note it on the
menu card.

**Plated** — add 20% to the plating time of shared table, and check the pass
can hold the cover count in one wave. Plated service is bound by pass space and
hands, not by food.

**Grazing** — weigh it out rather than eyeballing; quantity is the whole job.
Fruit, soft cheese and leaves die fast.

**Multi-day** — build a block per service, not per day. Order shared pantry
once for the stay. Confirm in writing which services the group might skip — the
food is carried either way. Check venue fridge and freezer capacity before
ordering; this is what bites on multi-day jobs.
