Welcome

Mudgee's home of good coffee

Coffee Kingdom started with a simple idea — the same great service and amazing coffee, wherever you are. Today we pour barista-made coffee from two friendly cafés in Mudgee, whip up fresh breakfasts, lunches and sweet treats, and take our mobile coffee cart on the road for events of every kind.

Whether you're grabbing a quick flat white on the way through, settling in for brekky, or planning the coffee for your big day — you're always welcome in the Kingdom.
