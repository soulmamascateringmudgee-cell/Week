Who we are

A gym built on grit.

Bomber Boxing is a community boxing gym in the heart of Mudgee. Whether you've never thrown a punch or you're chasing a title, our coaches meet you where you're at and push you further than you thought you could go.

From kids finding their confidence to amateurs stepping into the ring, everyone trains under the same roof, the same banner, and the same values.

What we offer

Classes for everyone.

Kids Boxing

Ages 5–11 & 11–17

Confidence, discipline and fitness in a safe, supportive environment. Non-contact fundamentals through to supervised skills.

Boxing / Fitness

All levels

High-energy boxing workouts that build cardio, coordination and power. No experience needed — just show up.

Strength & Conditioning

All levels

Build the engine behind the boxer. Strength, power and conditioning to keep you going every round.

Sparring

Experienced

Put your skills to the test in controlled, coached sparring sessions. Sharpen timing, defence and ring craft.

Fighters & Boxing Fitness

Competitive

Serious training for those chasing the ring. Fight prep, pad work and conditioning with our coaching team.

Open Gym

Members

Work the bags, skip, drill and train on your own time. The gym is yours to build in.
