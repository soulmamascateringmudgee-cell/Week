Good to Know

Frequently asked.

Where are you located?

Restore is at 85 Market Street, Mudgee — inside and upstairs of Mixi Blu. Head in and up the stairs, and you'll find us. Street parking is available nearby along Market Street.

How do I book?

The quickest way is online through the Book Now button — you'll see live availability and can secure your spot in a couple of taps. Payment is taken at the time of booking.

What's your cancellation policy?

Life happens — we just ask for at least 24 hours' notice if you need to change or cancel, so the time can be offered to someone else.

What should I expect at my first visit?

Arrive a few minutes early to settle in. Lauren will chat with you about what you're after and any areas of focus, then it's over to you to relax and enjoy.

Do you sell gift vouchers?

Yes — Restore vouchers can be used on any treatment and make a lovely gift. Just ask, or send an enquiry below and we'll sort one out for you.
